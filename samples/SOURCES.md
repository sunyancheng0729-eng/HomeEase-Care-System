# Sample Sources

`fall-01-cam0.mp4` and `fall-01-acc.csv` are the official UR Fall Detection
Dataset (URFD) fall-01 RGB camera-0 recording and accelerometer data.

Source: https://fenix.ur.edu.pl/~mkepski/ds/uf.html

Files:
- https://fenix.ur.edu.pl/~mkepski/ds/data/fall-01-cam0.mp4
- https://fenix.ur.edu.pl/~mkepski/ds/data/fall-01-acc.csv

These are reproducible replay inputs, not live household observations. The two
original detectors are invoked independently; the system does not invent a
fusion classifier. Follow the dataset authors' terms for redistribution and
publication, and cite their work as listed on the source page.

Exercise previews are rendered from the existing algorithm2 examples. Audio
examples are read directly from algorithm3/algorithm3_handover/samples.
